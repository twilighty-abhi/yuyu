If you can't remember your password, Yuyu can email you a link to set a new one. The link works once and only for an hour.

## Reset your password

<Steps>

1. On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot password?** above the password field.
2. Enter the **Email** you sign in with and choose **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and follow its link. It opens **Set a new password** for your address.
4. Enter a **New password** of at least 12 characters and choose **Save new password**.
5. Yuyu confirms "Password reset successfully!" and takes you back to sign in. Sign in with the new password.

</Steps>

After you ask for a link, Yuyu always says "If an account exists with that email, we've sent a password reset link." It says this whether or not the address has an account, so the form can't be used to find out who uses Yuyu.

## What a reset changes

- **You're signed out everywhere.** Every browser and the Yuyu mobile app are signed out, so anyone who had your old password loses access straight away.
- **Your email counts as verified.** Following the link proves you own the inbox, so an unverified account becomes verified.
- **Two-step verification stays on.** If you use an authenticator app, you still need a code (or a recovery code) when you next sign in. See [Two-step verification](/help/account/two-step-verification).
- **Older links stop working.** Each new request replaces the previous link, so always use the most recent email.

The change appears under **Account settings** → **Security** → **Recent security activity** as "Password reset by email link".

## If the email doesn't arrive

- Wait a few minutes and check your spam or junk folder.
- Make sure you typed the address you **sign in with**. A reset can't be sent to a different address.
- If you signed up with **Continue with Google** and never set a password, there is nothing to reset and no email is sent. Sign in with Google instead. Once signed in, you can add a password from **Account settings** → **Security** (see [Profile, appearance and security](/help/account/profile-and-security#password)).
- Asking many times in a row is limited. If you've tried several times, wait a while and use the latest email that arrives.

If nothing arrives at all, the instance may have an email delivery problem. See [Common problems](/help/troubleshooting/common-problems) or ask the person who runs your Yuyu instance.

## If the link doesn't work

"Invalid or expired reset link" means the link is more than an hour old, has already been used, or was replaced by a newer request. Go back to **Forgot password?** and ask for a fresh one.

<Callout kind="tip">

Already signed in and just want a different password? You don't need a reset link. Use **Account settings** → **Security** → **Password** instead.

</Callout>
