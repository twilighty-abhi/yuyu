Your account keeps track of the events you've registered for and the organisations you help run. From **Account settings** you can see all of it, download a copy of your personal data, and step away from organisations you no longer help with.

## Your registrations

The **Registrations** tab lists the events you registered for while signed in, in three sections:

- **Upcoming**: events that haven't ended yet, soonest first.
- **Past**: events that have ended, most recent first.
- **Certificates**: certificates from events where you gave feedback. Choose **View certificate** to download one.

Each registration shows its status (**Confirmed**, **Waitlisted**, **Waiting for approval**, **Declined** or **Checked in**) and buttons such as **View ticket**, **Event page**, **Manage team**, **Project** and **Cancel registration**, depending on the event. Long lists are split into pages of 50.

Registrations you made as a guest, without signing in, aren't listed here, even if you used the same email address. Use the links in their confirmation emails instead.

See [Your ticket](/help/attending/your-ticket) for what you can do with each ticket.

## Download your data

At the bottom of the **Registrations** tab, **Download my data** gives you a copy of what Yuyu holds about you, as a JSON file you can open in any text editor or import elsewhere.

<Steps>

1. Open **Account settings** → **Registrations**.
2. Scroll to **Download my data** and choose **Download my data**.
3. The file downloads straight away. Yuyu shows "Your download has started."

</Steps>

The file contains:

- your profile: name, email, whether it's verified, when you joined, whether two-step verification is on, and which sign-in methods are linked
- your organisation memberships and roles
- your registrations, with their status, check-in time, team, and your answers to registration questions
- hackathon projects from your registrations
- feedback you gave while matched to your registration (for certificates)
- your event co-organiser roles and permissions

Passwords, security and recovery codes, and ticket or other private links are never included. Anonymous feedback isn't linked to you, so it isn't in the file either.

<Callout kind="note">

You can download your data up to five times an hour. If you see "You've downloaded your data several times recently", try again later.

</Callout>

## Your organisations

The **Organisations** tab has three cards:

- **Your organisations**: each organisation you belong to, its web address, when you joined, your role, and how many upcoming events it has. **Open dashboard** takes you to it. **Create organisation** starts a new one.
- **Event co-organiser roles**: events and series where an organisation gave you co-organiser permissions, with the permissions you hold. **Open event** takes you to that event's dashboard.
- **Pending invitations**: co-organiser invitations sent to your email address that you haven't accepted yet. Accept them from the link in the invitation email. You need a verified email to see this list.

## Leave an organisation

<Steps>

1. Open **Account settings** → **Organisations**.
2. Next to the organisation, choose **Leave**.
3. Read the confirmation and choose **Leave organisation**.

</Steps>

You lose access to the organisation's dashboard, events and members straight away. To come back, an owner or admin has to invite you again. Leaving only removes your own membership: the organisation, its events and everyone else are untouched. Any event co-organiser roles you hold are kept, so you still reach those events.

<Callout kind="warning">

If you're the organisation's **only owner**, **Leave** is greyed out with "You're the only owner, so you can't leave yet." An organisation can't be left without an owner. Yuyu doesn't yet have a way to hand ownership to someone else in the app, so your options are to stay, or to delete the organisation from [Organisation settings](/help/organisations/settings).

</Callout>

## Deleting your account

You can't delete your account from the app yet. To have it deleted, contact the person who runs your Yuyu instance. Downloading your data first is a good idea.
