You can thank people for coming with a certificate. When certificates are on, guests who fill in your [feedback form](/help/after/feedback) can download a JPEG certificate with their name on it straight away. The certificate designer lets you use your own artwork and place the text and logos exactly where you want them.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins of the organisation.

</Callout>

## Turning certificates on

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Feedback** tab.
2. In the **Certificate** card, turn on the switch.
3. Choose **Design certificate** to open the designer.

</Steps>

Turning certificates on changes the feedback form: respondents must enter the **Email you registered with**, and it has to match a confirmed registration for the event. Feedback is no longer anonymous. See [Feedback forms](/help/after/feedback#anonymous-or-verified).

If you don't design anything, guests get the standard Yuyu certificate.

## Designing the certificate

<Screenshot name="certificate-designer" alt="The certificate designer with uploaded artwork, text layers for the attendee name and event title, and the layer settings" />

### Background

Choose **Upload background** and pick a JPEG, PNG or WebP image up to 5 MB, in landscape or portrait. For sharp printing, upload the original artwork at least 3508 pixels on its longest side, preferably as a PNG. Choose **Replace background** to change it, or **Use default certificate** to go back to the standard one.

<Callout kind="warning">

Leave personal details out of the background image. It's the same for everyone, and it's stored as an ordinary image.

</Callout>

### Layers

Build the certificate from up to 20 layers on top of the background:

- **Add text** for words: a title, the attendee's name, the event, a date, a signature line.
- **Add image** for logos, signatures or seals: JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB. Transparent PNG backgrounds stay transparent, and images keep their shape inside their box.

### Values that change per certificate

In a text layer, use **Insert a value that changes per certificate** to add:

| Value | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Attendee name** | The guest's registered name. |
| **Event title** | Your event's title. |
| **Event date** | The event's date. |
| **Organisation** | Your organisation's name. |
| **Issue date** | The date the certificate was issued. |

Dates use the event's time zone. You can mix values with your own words, such as "Awarded to" followed by the attendee's name. If no layer includes **Attendee name**, the designer warns you, because every certificate would look the same.

### Styling text

For each text layer, choose the **Font** (**Classic serif**, **Clean sans** or **Monospace**), **Size**, **Colour**, **Letter spacing** and alignment, and switch **Bold**, **Italic** or **Uppercase** on or off. Line breaks are kept. Long text, such as a long name, shrinks automatically to fit its box. Give a layer a **Label** to find it easily in the layer list.

### Arranging layers

- Click a layer to select it, drag it to move it (it snaps to the centre), and drag the corner handle to resize it.
- Nudge the selected layer with the arrow keys. Hold <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> for bigger steps.
- Use **Centre horizontally** and **Centre vertically**, or type exact positions and sizes as percentages.
- In **Layers**, bring a layer forward or send it backward, duplicate it, or delete it.

## Previewing and saving

The layout preview uses your browser's fonts, so it can differ slightly from the real thing. To check exactly what guests will get:

<Steps>

1. Under **Sample values**, type a long name into **Preview attendee name**.
2. Choose **Generate exact preview**. Yuyu draws the certificate exactly as it will be downloaded.
3. If the name fits and everything lines up, choose **Save changes**.

</Steps>

The design isn't used until you save it.

## What guests get

After sending the feedback form, a guest chooses **Download certificate (JPG)**. Guests with an account can also find their certificates later under **Certificates** on their account's registrations page. See [Feedback and certificates](/help/attending/feedback-and-certificates).

Each response keeps the design that was active when it was sent. If you change the design later, only new certificates use it.

## Good to know

- A certificate is only issued to someone whose email matches a confirmed registration. They don't need to have been checked in.
- Matching an email isn't proof that the person owns that inbox. For certificates with real-world value, such as course credits, check them against your own records.
- Turning certificates off makes new feedback anonymous again. Certificates already issued can still be downloaded, unless that guest's registration is later cancelled or declined.
