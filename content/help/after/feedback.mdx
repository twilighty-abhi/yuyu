A feedback form tells you what worked and what to change next time. Each event has its own form on the **Feedback** tab. You choose the questions, open the form when you're ready, and share the link with your guests. Responses are summarised for you, without names.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins of the organisation. Co-organisers can't set up feedback or see responses.

</Callout>

## Setting up the form

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Feedback** tab.
2. Choose **Use a template** and pick one, or choose **Add question** to start from scratch.
3. Change the form's title with the pencil button next to it, if you like. It starts as "Event feedback".
4. Under **After they submit**, write the **Thank-you message** guests see after sending.
5. Choose **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom.

</Steps>

### Templates

| Template | What it asks |
| --- | --- |
| **Quick pulse** | An overall rating and one optional comment. |
| **Event review** | Overall rating, what was most valuable, how well it was organised, whether they'd recommend it, and what to improve. |
| **Session & speaker review** | Content quality, how clear the speakers were, a key takeaway, and topics for next time. |

A template replaces any questions nobody has answered yet, and closes the form so you can review it before guests see it. You can't apply a template once the form has responses.

### Questions

Choose **Add question**, write the **Question**, pick an **Answer type** and tick **Required** if it must be answered. The answer types are **Short answer**, **Long answer**, **Dropdown**, **Pick several**, **Pick one**, **Yes / no** and **Number**. For a dropdown or a pick list, type the choices in **Options (comma separated)**. A form can have up to 100 questions.

<Callout kind="tip">

For a rating scale, use **Pick one** or **Dropdown** with numbers such as 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, or words such as Excellent, Good, Okay, Poor. Yuyu then shows a bar chart for that question, with an average for number scales.

</Callout>

Once a question has answers, you can still reword it, but you can't change its answer type or options, and you can't delete it. That keeps earlier responses meaning what people meant.

## Opening and sharing the form

The form is closed until you open it. When you're ready, usually at the end of the event:

<Steps>

1. Make sure the form has at least one question.
2. Turn the switch at the top of the **Feedback** tab to **Open**.
3. Choose **Copy feedback link**.
4. Share the link with your guests.

</Steps>

<Callout kind="note">

Yuyu never sends the feedback link by itself. Opening the form doesn't email anyone. Share the link yourself: paste it into a chat group or onto a slide, or send the **Thank you and feedback** email template from the **Emails** tab, which links to the form for you. See [Emailing your guests](/help/guests/emailing-guests).

</Callout>

The form is only available while the event is live. Turn the switch back to **Closed** to stop taking responses.

## Anonymous or verified

What respondents are asked depends on whether you offer certificates:

- **Certificates off.** Feedback is anonymous. Nobody is asked for an email address, and responses aren't linked to a registration. People can respond more than once.
- **Certificates on.** Respondents enter the **Email you registered with**, which must match a confirmed registration for the event. After sending, they can download a certificate with their name on it. See [The certificate designer](/help/after/certificates).

Choose anonymous feedback if honest answers matter most, and certificates if you want to reward people for coming.

## Reading responses

The **Responses** card at the top of the tab shows how many responses there are, always without names:

- Rating questions show how many people chose each answer, with the average for number scales or the most common answer for word scales.
- Written answers show the most recent few, with a link to read them all.
- **Show individual responses** lists every response, 25 at a time.

Choose **Download CSV** to get every response in a spreadsheet. Downloads are recorded in the organisation's audit log.

## Good to know

- Responses also feed the **Gave feedback** step on the **Analytics** tab and the post-event report. See [Analytics](/help/after/analytics).
- Guests' view of the form is described in [Feedback and certificates](/help/attending/feedback-and-certificates).
