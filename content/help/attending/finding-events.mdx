There are four ways to find an event on Yuyu: browse **Discover**, visit the organisation's own page, follow a short link, or open the link an organiser sent you. All events on Yuyu are free to attend.

## Browse Discover

**Discover** lists every public event on this Yuyu instance that hasn't ended yet. Open it from **Discover** in the site header.

<Steps>

1. Type a word or two into **Search events** and choose **Search**. Search looks at event titles and descriptions.
2. Narrow by date with **All**, **This week** or **This month**, or choose **Pick dates**, set **From** and **To**, and choose **Apply dates**.
3. Switch the order between **Upcoming** (soonest first) and **Popular** (most people going).
4. Choose an event card to open its event page.

</Steps>

<Screenshot name="discover-page" alt="The Discover page with the search box, date filters and a grid of event cards" />

Results come 12 to a page. To start again, choose the **×** (**Clear search**) in the search box.

Dates of a recurring series appear individually, so a weekly meetup shows one card for each upcoming date.

## Organisation pages

Every organisation has a public page at its own address, such as `example.org/your-community`. It shows the organisation's description and its events in two tabs, **Upcoming** and **Past**, as a **List** or a **Calendar**.

To get there, choose the **Hosted by** box on any of the organisation's event pages, or ask the organisers for the address.

## Short links and shared links

Organisers can give an event a short link made of seven letters and numbers, such as `example.org/aB3x9Kq`, which is easy to print on a poster or read out. It takes you straight to the event page. Short links work only while the event is Live; otherwise you see a "not found" page.

Organisers can also share the event's full address directly, by email, chat or social media.

## Why can't I find an event?

Not every event is listed. Organisers choose who can find each one:

| How the organisers set it up | Discover and organisation page | Opening the link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Everyone** | Listed | Works for anyone |
| **People with the link** | Not listed | Works for anyone with the link |
| **Invited people only** | Not listed | Works only when you're signed in with an invited, verified email address |

Events that need approval can be listed or unlisted, depending on the organisers' choice.

You also won't find an event that:

- is still a **Draft** or has been taken **Offline** by its organisers
- has already ended (it still appears under **Past** on the organisation page)

If a link you were sent shows "not found", check you copied all of it. For an invite-only event, [sign in](/help/account/sign-up-and-sign-in) with the email address the invitation was sent to, and make sure that address is verified. Otherwise, ask the organisers whether the event is live and who it's open to.
