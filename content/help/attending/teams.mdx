Hackathons, game jams and competitions often register people in teams. On a team event, one person creates the team and becomes its **leader**, and teammates join with the team's link. Each team takes one place, however many members it has.

## How team registration works

When you register for a team event, the form asks **How are you taking part?**

- **Create a team**: you lead the team and get a link to invite teammates.
- **Join with a link**: your leader shares a link that registers you straight into their team.
- **Register solo**: take part on your own. This appears only when the organisers allow it.

The form also shows the team size limits, for example the most members a team can have. If the organisers set a minimum size, a team below it is marked **Incomplete** and can't be approved until it's big enough.

Everyone in a team shares the team's status. If the event needs approval, or is full, the whole team waits together, and when the organisers approve the team or give it a place, every member is confirmed at once and gets their own ticket by email.

## Create a team

<Steps>

1. On the event page, choose **Register your team** (or **Register a team or yourself**).
2. Choose **Create a team** and enter a **Team name** (up to 60 characters). It must be different from the other teams' names at this event.
3. Fill in your details and any questions, then choose **Register**.
4. Check your email. Alongside your ticket, you get a link to your private **team page**, where you'll find the join link to share.

</Steps>

If you're signed in, you can also reach the team page from **Account settings** → **Registrations** → **Manage team**, or **Manage team** on your ticket.

## Join a team

Ask your team leader for the team's join link. Opening it shows **You're joining** with the team's name and size, and the usual registration form. Fill it in and choose **Register**.

**Already registered on your own?** Don't register again. Either:

- open the join link while signed in to the account you registered with, or in the browser you registered in, and choose **Join** followed by the team's name, or
- open your ticket, find **Join a team**, paste the link into **Team link** and choose **Join this team**.

You keep your ticket. Your place then follows the team's: if the team is still waiting for approval or a place, you wait with it.

You can only be in one team per event. A full team, or one the organisers declined, can't be joined.

## Find a team

On some events, the organisers let people who register solo look for a team.

<Steps>

1. When registering, choose **Register solo** and tick **List me as looking for a team**.
2. Add a **Headline** about what you bring (up to 120 characters) and up to 8 **Skills**.
3. After registering, open your ticket. The **Looking for a team** card shows **Teams looking for members**, where you can choose **Ask to join**, and **Others looking for a team**.

</Steps>

Team leaders can also invite you; invitations appear on your ticket with **Join team** and **Decline**. Other people only ever see your first name, last initial, headline and skills, never your email. Use **Edit details** to change your profile or **Show me on the list** to hide yourself. Nobody is emailed about requests and invitations, so check your ticket now and then.

## Manage your team

Your team page is for leaders. Anyone with its link can manage the team, so keep it private.

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Rename the team | **Rename**, enter the new name, then **Save**. |
| Share the join link | **Copy** in **Join link**. |
| Stop an old join link working | **Create a new link…**. The old link stops at once. |
| Add someone yourself | Under **Add a member**, enter their **Name** and **Email** and choose **Add member**. They're emailed their own ticket and don't fill in the form. |
| Remove a member | Their **⋯** menu → **Remove from team…**. Their registration is cancelled. |
| Hand over leadership | Their **⋯** menu → **Make leader…**. They get a new team page link by email, and your link stops working. You stay in the team. |
| Find teammates | Turn on **Looking for members** in **Find teammates**, then **Accept** or **Decline** requests, or **Invite** people looking for a team. |
| Secure the page | **Reset management link…** under **This page's link**. The new link is emailed to you. |
| Disband the team | **Disband team…**. Everyone's registration is cancelled and the team is deleted. This can't be undone. |

Adding members isn't possible on invite-only events (share the join link instead; each person signs in with their invited account), once registration has closed, or when the team is full.

If the organisers decline your team, you can still view it, remove members or disband it, but you can't add people or change it.

## Leave a team

<Steps>

1. Open your ticket and choose **Leave team** in your team's card.
2. Confirm with **Leave team**.

</Steps>

Leaving cancels your registration for the event. You can then register again on your own or join another team. If the leader leaves, leadership passes to the member who joined first; if they were the only member, the team is deleted. You can't leave after you've been checked in.

## Good to know

- Recurring series don't use teams.
- Teammates see each other's names on their tickets, but never each other's emails.
- To submit your team's hackathon project, see [Submitting a hackathon project](/help/attending/submitting-a-project).
