Yuyu can print a name badge for each guest the moment they're checked in. You design the badge once, connect a printer to the check-in device, and every check-in brings up that guest's badge ready to print. Badges print in black and white, so they work on ordinary laser printers and thermal label printers alike.

<Callout kind="permission">

Anyone who can use the [check-in desk](/help/check-in/check-in-desk) (owners, admins and co-organisers with the **Check-in** permission), and volunteers at the [door station](/help/check-in/door-station).

</Callout>

## Turning on badge printing

<Steps>

1. Open the check-in desk on the device connected to your printer.
2. Turn on **ID card printing**, next to **Override waitlist**.
3. Choose **Set up cards** to open the badge designer and get the design right (see below).
4. Check someone in. A sheet opens with their badge.
5. Choose **Print badge** or press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>. Choose **Skip** if they don't need one.

</Steps>

**ID card printing** is off by default and only applies to the desk you're on, so turn it on at each desk that has a printer. It switches off again if you reload the page.

After printing, the sheet offers **Print again** in case the badge didn't come out, and **Done**. To reprint later, choose **Print ID card** on the guest's check-in result.

<Callout kind="tip">

In your browser's print settings, print at 100% (actual size), with headers and footers turned off. If nothing happens when you print, allow pop-ups for this site: badges print from a separate window.

</Callout>

## Designing the badge

Choose **Set up cards** on the desk, or **Edit design** on a print sheet, to open the **Badge designer**. It has three parts: settings on the left, a live preview in the middle, and the selected item's properties on the right.

<Screenshot name="badge-designer" alt="The badge designer with style, paper and text settings, a live badge preview and the selected item panel" />

**Style.** Pick **Classic** (a thin frame, with a hairline under the heading), **Bold** (the heading on a black band, the name in capitals) or **Minimal** (no lines, just the name). The guest's name is always the largest text. Changing style resets item positions.

**Paper.** Choose your **Badge stock**: **A6**, **4 × 6 in**, **3 × 4 in**, **4 × 3 in**, **ID card (CR80)**, or **Custom size** with a width and height between 40 and 300 mm. Pick **Portrait** or **Landscape**. Text and spacing scale to fit the stock.

**Text.** The **Heading** starts as the event title. The **Badge label** (such as "ATTENDEE" or "SPEAKER") and the **Footer** (your organisation's name to begin with) each have a switch to hide them.

**Show on the badge.** Switch **Attendee email** and **Organisation logo** on or off. The logo comes from your organisation settings. **Shrink long names to fit** keeps long names on one line, down to 60% of the normal size.

**Registration answers.** Print answers from your sign-up form, such as company or role, under the name. Add them from **Add an answer**, put them in order, and give each a shorter printed label if you like. On team events you can also add the guest's **Team**.

### Arranging items

Click an item on the preview to select it, then drag it, or nudge it with the arrow keys (hold <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> for bigger steps). In the right-hand panel you can set the text size, box width and exact position in millimetres, align it on the badge, or choose **Reset this item**. Use the **Snap** control (for example **Snap 5 mm**) to pick the grid size, and the guides button to show or hide the grid. Undo and redo are in the top corner.

Switch the preview between **Short name** and **Long name**, so you know the longest names in your guest list will fit.

Every badge carries the guest's own check-in QR code, made on the device, so the badge can be scanned later. Keep the QR code at least 20 mm across so it scans reliably once printed.

## Testing and sharing the design

- **Print test card** prints a badge with sample details. Do this before the doors open.
- The design is saved on this device, for this event, as you go. Another device starts with the default design.
- To set up a second desk the same way, choose **Export** to save the design as a file, then **Import** it on the other device.

## Good to know

- Badges also print when you're checking in offline. See [Checking in offline](/help/check-in/offline).
- Guests' answers only print if you choose them under **Registration answers**. Nothing else from the sign-up form appears on the badge.
