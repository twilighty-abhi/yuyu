The check-in desk is where you let guests in on the day. It runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or laptop, so you don't need special hardware: scan the QR code on a guest's ticket, paste their ticket link, or find them by name.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins of the organisation, and co-organisers with the **Check-in** permission for the event. Volunteers without a Yuyu account can use the [door station](/help/check-in/door-station) instead.

</Callout>

## Opening the desk

<Steps>

1. Open the event from your dashboard and choose the **Check-in** tab.
2. Choose **Open check-in**.
3. On a phone or tablet, choose **Start camera** under **Scan QR** and allow camera access when the browser asks.

</Steps>

The **Check-in** tab also shows when doors open (for example "Doors open in 2 hours", then "Happening now"), how many guests have arrived out of everyone going, and a short reminder of who gets in at the door.

<Screenshot name="check-in-desk" alt="The check-in desk with the camera scanner, manual code box and attendee search" />

At the top of the desk you'll see how many confirmed guests are checked in, as a number and a percentage, and whether this device is **Online** or **Offline**.

## Three ways to check someone in

**Scan QR.** Point the camera at the QR code on the guest's ticket. The code only holds a secret ticket link, never personal details.

**Manual code.** If a screen is cracked or the camera won't start, paste the guest's ticket link or code into the **Manual code** box and choose **Preview**.

**Search attendees.** Type at least two letters of a name or email address (or a team name, on team events) and choose **Search**. Up to 12 matches appear, each with their status. Choose **Check in** next to the right person. Choose a name to see their **Attendee details**, including their registration answers.

Whichever way you use, Yuyu shows a **Confirm check-in** box with the guest's name, email, team and status. Check it's the right person, then choose **Confirm** or press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>. The desk beeps, the phone buzzes, and a large **Checked in** panel shows the guest's name. Choose **Next guest** to clear it.

<Callout kind="tip">

Answers to sign-up questions about food or dietary preferences and T-shirt sizes show automatically when you check someone in, so you can hand out the right meal ticket or shirt straight away.

</Callout>

If you asked guests whether they're still coming, the confirm box, search results and attendee details show their answer as a chip (**Coming**, **Not coming** or **No reply**). It's for information only: a guest who said No but still has a confirmed place checks in as normal. See [Attendance confirmation](/help/guests/attendance-confirmation).

Crew members check in the same way. Their confirm box and details show a **Crew** chip and their food preference, and the **Checked in** panel says **Event crew**. See [Event crew](/help/guests/event-crew).

If the guest's ticket page is open on their phone, it updates by itself to show they're checked in.

## Who gets in

The desk follows the guest's registration status:

| Status | At the door |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | Checked in straight away. |
| **Waitlisted** | Blocked unless you turn on **Override waitlist**. |
| **Pending approval** | Blocked. Approve them in the **Guests** tab first. |
| **Rejected** or **Cancelled** | Blocked, with the reason shown. |

**Override waitlist** is a switch above the scanner. It only lets waitlisted guests in; people waiting for approval and declined registrations stay blocked even with it on. Turn it off again when you're done so nobody slips through by accident.

A ticket that has already been used shows **Already checked in** with the time, so a shared screenshot can't get a second person in.

## Fixing mistakes

Checked in the wrong person? Find them under **Recent check-ins** at the bottom of the desk and choose **Undo** (on a phone, **Undo check-in**). You can also search for them and choose **Undo** in the results. Undo needs a connection.

**Recent check-ins** lists arrivals newest first. Choose **Load more** to see older ones. The desk refreshes itself every 30 seconds while it's open, so several desks at the same door stay in step.

Every check-in and undo is recorded with who did it and when. Nothing is overwritten.

## Downloading attendance

People who can manage the guest list see **Download attendee CSV** on the desk, and **Export attendance** on the **Check-in** tab. The file lists everyone who registered, with their check-in times. See [Exporting guest data](/help/guests/exports).

## Good to know

- Keep the desk working when the Wi-Fi drops by saving the guest list first. See [Checking in offline](/help/check-in/offline).
- To print a name badge as each guest arrives, turn on **ID card printing**. See [Printing badges](/help/check-in/badges).
- To track swag, meals or workshops as well as entry, see [Custom check-in lists](/help/check-in/check-in-lists).
- Guests who can't find their ticket can open it from their confirmation email. See [Your ticket](/help/attending/your-ticket).
