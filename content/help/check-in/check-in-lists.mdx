Entry isn't the only thing you hand out on the day. Custom check-in lists let you track swag, meals, workshops or anything else alongside entry: "Swag collection", "Lunch – Day 1", "Workshop B". Each list records who has collected it, separately from whether they came through the door.

<Callout kind="permission">

Setting up lists (adding, renaming, reordering, archiving): owners and admins, and co-organisers with the **Edit details** permission.

Checking guests into a list: owners and admins, and co-organisers with the **Check-in** permission, the same people who run the door.

</Callout>

## Setting up a list

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Check-in** tab.
2. In the **Check-in lists** card, choose **Manage lists**.
3. Under **New list**, type a name such as "Swag collection".
4. Turn on **Only after entry check-in** if guests must come through the door first, for example so nobody collects a lunch voucher without arriving.
5. Choose **Add list**, then **Done**.

</Steps>

An event can have up to 30 lists, and each name can be up to 60 characters. Two open lists can't share a name.

The **Check-in lists** card then shows each list with how many guests have collected it out of those eligible, such as "42 / 120 collected".

## Checking guests into a list

<Steps>

1. On the **Check-in** tab, choose **Open** next to the list. Or open the check-in desk and choose the list from the tabs at the top: **Entry** is the normal door desk, followed by each of your lists.
2. Scan a ticket, paste a ticket link or code under **Manual code**, or use **Search guests**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot name="check-in-list-desk" alt="A custom check-in list desk with the list tabs, scanner, search and the guests filtered by collected or not collected" />

Unlike entry, a list doesn't ask you to confirm: each scan marks the guest for the list straight away, with a beep and a buzz. What you see next depends on the guest:

| You see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "Checked in to Swag at 10:42" | Done. Choose **Next guest**, or **Undo** if it was a mistake. |
| "Already checked in to Swag at 10:42" | They've already collected it. Nothing changes. |
| **On the waitlist** | The guest is waitlisted. Choose **Admit anyway** to add them, or **Scan again**. |
| "Not added to Swag" | They aren't eligible, with the reason, for example they haven't been checked in at entry yet. |

The eligibility rules are the same as at the door: confirmed guests are accepted, waitlisted guests need **Override waitlist** (or **Admit anyway**), and guests waiting for approval or declined are refused.

## Seeing who has collected

Under **Guests for** your list, you'll find confirmed guests plus anyone already in the list, in the order they registered. Use **All**, **Collected** and **Not collected** to filter, or type in **Filter by name or email**. Each guest has **Check in** or **Undo** next to them. Choose **Show more** to load more guests.

A guest marked "Not checked in at entry" can't collect from a list that needs entry check-in first.

## Renaming, reordering and archiving

Open **Manage lists** again to:

- change a list's name and choose **Save name**
- move lists up or down, which changes the order of the tabs on the desk
- switch **Only after entry check-in** on or off
- choose **Archive** when a list is finished

Archived lists keep their check-ins and stay in the attendee export, but they're read-only and hidden from the desk. Choose **Restore** under **Archived** to bring one back. Lists can't be deleted.

## Where the results show up

- The **Guests** tab shows the lists each guest has collected.
- The attendee CSV has a "Check-in:" column for each list, with the time or blank. See [Exporting guest data](/help/guests/exports).

## Good to know

- Checking into a list never changes a guest's entry check-in or their ticket.
- Lists need a connection. They aren't available in [offline mode](/help/check-in/offline) or at the [door station](/help/check-in/door-station).
- Lists are for one-off events. They aren't available on the dates of a [recurring series](/help/events/recurring-series).
