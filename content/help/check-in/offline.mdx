Venue Wi-Fi often struggles when a crowd arrives. The check-in desk can keep working without a connection: you save the guest list to the device beforehand, check people in as usual, and the check-ins upload when the connection comes back.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins, and co-organisers with the **Check-in** permission. Offline mode is part of the signed-in check-in desk only. The [door station](/help/check-in/door-station) always needs a connection.

</Callout>

## Before the doors open

Do this on every phone or tablet you'll use at the door, while you still have a good connection.

<Steps>

1. Sign in and open the event's **Check-in** tab, then choose **Open check-in**.
2. In the box near the top, choose **Make available offline**.
3. Wait for "Offline check-in ready" and the number of attendees saved on this device.
4. If the browser offers it, choose **Install check-in app** to add the desk to the device's home screen.
5. Leave the check-in page open. Don't reload it while you're offline.

</Steps>

<Callout kind="warning">

The browser can't load the check-in page from scratch without a connection. Open it while you're online and keep it open. If you close or reload it while offline, you won't get it back until the connection returns.

</Callout>

If people register or are approved after you saved the list, choose **Refresh roster** while you're online to update it.

## Checking in without a connection

When the device loses its connection, the chip at the top of the desk changes from **Online** to **Offline**. Carry on as usual:

- **Scan QR** and **Manual code** look the ticket up in the saved list.
- **Search attendees** searches the saved list by name, email or team.
- The **Confirm check-in** box and **Override waitlist** work the same way as online.

Each check-in is saved on the device with the time it happened, and the box at the top counts how many are waiting to sync.

A few things wait for a connection:

- **Undo** is turned off while offline. Undo mistakes once you're back online.
- Each device only knows about its own check-ins. If two devices are offline at once, a guest could be checked in on both. When they sync, whichever device syncs first records the check-in, and the other is treated as already checked in.
- A ticket that isn't in the saved list (for example, someone who registered after you saved it) can't be checked in until you're online.

## Syncing when you're back online

When the connection returns, the device uploads its waiting check-ins by itself. You can also choose **Sync** (the button shows how many are waiting). Every check-in keeps the time it actually happened, not the time it synced.

If some check-ins can't be accepted, you'll see a message that they "need attention". This happens when, for example, a guest's registration was declined in the meantime, or the check-in was recorded outside the event's window (from 24 hours before the start to one hour after the end). Check those guests on the **Guests** tab.

## Cleaning up afterwards

The saved list holds guests' names, email addresses and ticket links, so don't leave it on a shared device.

- It expires by itself 24 hours after you saved it.
- To remove it sooner, choose **Remove offline data**. Sync first: the button stays unavailable while check-ins are waiting to upload.

## Good to know

- Offline mode covers entry check-in only. [Custom check-in lists](/help/check-in/check-in-lists) need a connection.
- Badges still print offline if you've turned on **ID card printing**. The QR code on each badge is made on the device. See [Printing badges](/help/check-in/badges).
- For everything else about the desk, see [The check-in desk](/help/check-in/check-in-desk).
