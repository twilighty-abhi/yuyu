Every event has its own public page: the cover, title and register button at the top, followed by sections such as About, Programme, Speakers, Venue and FAQ. You build it from the event dashboard, and guests see it once the event is live.

## Where to edit your page

Open the event and choose the **Event page** tab. It has three sub-tabs:

| Sub-tab | What you change there |
| --- | --- |
| **Details** | Title, tagline, logo, the **About the event** text, cover image, dates, location and publishing. See [Event details](/help/events/details). |
| **Page & programme** | The page colour, which sections show, and the content inside them: highlights, speakers, sponsors, resources and FAQs. |
| **Registration form** | The questions guests answer when they sign up. See [The registration form](/help/registration/form). |

**Page & programme** opens with a **Programme** card showing how many sessions, tracks and speakers you have, with **Open schedule** to build the programme. Use **Open public page** at the top to see the page as guests do; it opens in a new tab.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins, and co-organisers with the **Edit details** permission, can edit page content. The **Details** sub-tab and the **Registration form** are for owners and admins only. **Open schedule** needs the **Publish and schedule** permission.

</Callout>

## Sections

The **Sections** card lists every part of the page with a short note of what is in it, for example "3 questions · 1 not published" or "Empty".

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Hero** | Cover, title and register button. It always shows. |
| **About** | The **About the event** text from **Details**. |
| **Highlights** | Short selling points, each with an optional icon or emoji. |
| **Programme** | Your published sessions, with a link to the full programme. The public page titles this section **Schedule**. See [Building the programme](/help/event-page/programme). |
| **Speakers** | Speaker cards that link to each speaker's own page. |
| **Sponsors** | Sponsors with their logo and tier. The public page titles this section **Partners**. |
| **Venue** | The location and map link from **Details**, with a map. Not shown for online events. |
| **FAQ** | Questions and answers. |
| **Resources** | Links to slides, guides or anything else guests should open. |

Use the switch on a row to show or hide that section. Next to each row is a shortcut: **Add** or **Edit** jumps to the section's content further down, **Edit in Details** opens the Details sub-tab, and **Open schedule** opens the programme.

Changes to the colour and the section switches aren't saved straight away. A bar appears at the bottom of the screen with the number of unsaved changes: choose **Save changes**, or **Discard** to go back.

<Callout kind="note">

A section with nothing published in it stays off the public page even when its switch is on, so you can switch everything on and fill sections in as you go.

</Callout>

## Adding highlights, FAQs and resources

Highlights, speakers, sponsors, resources and FAQs each have a card under **Section content**. Every item is saved on its own, so you don't need the bottom bar for them.

<Steps>

1. Under **Section content**, find the right card and choose **Add Highlight**, **Add FAQ** or **Add Resource**.
2. Fill in the form:
   - A highlight has a **Name**, an optional **Icon** (a short icon or emoji) and a **Description**.
   - An FAQ has a **Question** and an **Answer**, which you can format with the text toolbar.
   - A resource has a **Name**, a **Resource link** (the public web address guests open) and a **Description**.
3. Choose its **Visibility** (see below).
4. Choose **Add Highlight**, **Add FAQ** or **Add Resource** at the bottom of the form.

</Steps>

To change an item, choose **Edit** on its row and then **Save changes**. **Remove** deletes it after you confirm; this can't be undone.

Speakers and sponsors work the same way and have a few more fields. See [Speakers and sponsors](/help/event-page/speakers-and-sponsors).

## Published, Draft and Hidden

Every item, and every programme session, has a **Visibility**:

- **Published**: shown on the public page.
- **Draft**: kept off the public page while you work on it.
- **Hidden**: kept off the public page, for something you've taken down but may want back.

Draft and Hidden look the same to guests: the item simply isn't there. Each row shows its visibility as a chip, so you can see at a glance what is live.

## Colour

The **Colour** card sets the accent used for the hero band, buttons and links on the event page. Choose one of the presets, or **Custom** to pick any colour, then choose **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom.

## Good to know

- You can build the whole page while the event is a draft. Guests can't see it until the event is live; see [Publishing and visibility](/help/events/publishing).
- While the event is a draft, people who can edit the page or the programme can still open the public page to preview it. Draft and Hidden items stay out of the preview too.
- The arrows on each section row change the order of the list, but the public page currently shows sections in a fixed order: About, Highlights, Schedule, Speakers, Partners, Venue, FAQ, Resources, Crew. The Crew section is managed from **Guests** → **Crew**; see [Event crew](/help/guests/event-crew).
- Items appear on the page in the order you added them.
