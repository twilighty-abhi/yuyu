Talks overrun and lunch takes longer than planned. Instead of editing every later session by hand, add a delay to the session that's running over: Yuyu moves everything after it in the same track, and the public programme shows the new times.

## How delays work

Each session keeps its **planned** times, and Yuyu works out **live** times from the delays you add:

- A delay on a session moves every **later** session in the **same track** back by that many minutes. The session itself keeps its times.
- Delays add up. A 10-minute delay on the morning keynote and a 5-minute delay on the next talk put everything after that talk 15 minutes behind.
- Other tracks aren't affected. If the main hall runs late, the workshop room stays on time.
- Planned times never change, so you can remove a delay at any point and the programme goes straight back.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins, and co-organisers with the **Publish and schedule** permission.

</Callout>

## Adding a delay

<Steps>

1. Open the event, choose the **Event page** tab, then **Page & programme**, and choose **Open schedule**.
2. Find the session that's running over in its track.
3. In **Delay from this session (min)**, type how many minutes late it will finish, for example 15.
4. Click or tap outside the box. The delay saves as soon as you leave it.

</Steps>

Every later session in that track now shows two lines: **Planned**, with the original times, and **Live**, with the new times and how far behind it is, for example "(15 min behind)".

<Screenshot name="programme-live-delay" alt="A track on the Programme schedule page with a 15-minute delay and the later sessions showing their live times" />

Delays are whole minutes from 0 to 1,440 (one day). You can't enter a negative delay to bring sessions forward; reduce or clear an earlier delay instead.

## Clearing a delay

Set **Delay from this session (min)** back to 0 and leave the box. Later sessions return to their planned times, less any other delays still in place.

## What guests see

- The **Programme** page shows the live times, and each delayed session carries a note such as **Running 15 min late**.
- The event page's **Schedule** section shows the live times.
- Each session's own page shows its live time, and its "next in this track" and "also at" lists use live times too.

Guests see the new times the next time they open or refresh a page. Yuyu doesn't email anyone about a delay. If you want to tell people, send a quick message from the **Emails** tab; the **Live update on the day** template is written for this. See [Emailing your guests](/help/guests/emailing-guests).

<Callout kind="tip">

Keep the schedule open on a phone during the event. One person updating the delay on the overrunning session is enough to keep every later time on the public programme right.

</Callout>

## Good to know

- Add the delay to a **Published** session. Public times are worked out from published sessions only, so a delay on a Draft or Hidden session doesn't move anything guests can see.
- A delay never changes the session's planned times. If the day is permanently rearranged, edit the sessions instead with **Edit** and new **Planned start** and **Planned end** times; see [Building the programme](/help/event-page/programme#adding-a-session).
- Overlap warnings in the schedule are based on planned times, not live ones.
