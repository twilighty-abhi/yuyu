The programme is your event's public running order: talks, workshops, breaks and anything else, arranged in tracks. Guests see it on the event page, on a full **Programme** page, and on a page for each session and speaker.

## Tracks and sessions

A **track** is one independent room, stage or stream, such as "Main Hall" or "Workshop room". Every session belongs to one track. A small event usually needs a single track; a conference with parallel rooms has one track per room.

On the public **Programme** page, tracks appear side by side, each with its sessions in time order. All times are shown in the event's time zone.

## Opening the schedule

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Event page** tab, then **Page & programme**.
2. On the **Programme** card, choose **Open schedule**.

</Steps>

The **Programme schedule** page has a **Programme tracks** card at the top and a card for each track below it. Use **Back to event** to return to the dashboard.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins, and co-organisers with the **Publish and schedule** permission. Despite its name, that permission doesn't let a co-organiser set the event live.

</Callout>

## Adding tracks

<Steps>

1. Under **Programme tracks**, type a name in **New track**, for example "Main Hall".
2. Choose **Add track**.

</Steps>

Each track has its own row where you can edit the name and choose **Rename**, move it with **Up** and **Down**, or **Remove** it. The order here is the order tracks appear in the programme. Track names must be unique within the event, and you can only remove a track once it has no sessions.

## Adding a session

<Steps>

1. On the track's card, choose **Add session**.
2. Under **Session details**, enter the **Session title** and, if you like, a **Session description**. The description appears on the session's own page.
3. Under **When and where**, set **Planned start** and **Planned end** in the event's time zone. The start is filled in for you with the end of the track's last session.
4. Check the **Track**, and pick a **Type**: Keynote, Talk, Panel, Workshop, Fireside chat, Networking, Break or Other. You can also type your own.
5. Under **Speakers and visibility**, select everyone appearing in the session.
6. Choose a **Visibility**: **Published** shows the session publicly; **Draft** and **Hidden** keep it off the public pages.
7. Choose **Add session**.

</Steps>

To change a session, choose **Edit**, make your changes and choose **Save changes**. **Remove** deletes the session after you confirm; this can't be undone.

### Sessions at the same time

Sessions in different tracks can run at the same time; that's what tracks are for. If two sessions in the **same** track overlap, Yuyu still saves them but shows a **Time overlap** chip and a warning, because guests may need guidance. When two sessions share a start time, **Display order** decides which is listed first.

## What guests see

- The event page's **Schedule** section lists your published sessions and links to the full programme.
- The **Programme** page shows every track with its published sessions, their type and their speakers.
- Each session has its own page with its time, description and speakers, what's next in the same track, and what else starts at the same time.
- Each speaker's page lists their sessions.

If you add a delay on the day, guests see the updated times and a "Running late" note. See [Running late: live delays](/help/event-page/live-delays).

## Good to know

- The Schedule section only appears on the event page once at least one session is published and the **Programme** section is switched on. See [Building your event page](/help/event-page/building-your-page#sections).
- Programme times use the event's time zone, set in [Event details](/help/events/details). If you pick a time that doesn't exist in that zone (for example during a clock change), Yuyu asks you to choose another.
- Recurring series have their own schedule on the series page; this article covers one-off events. See [Recurring series](/help/events/recurring-series).
