Speakers and sponsors are two sections of your event page. Each speaker gets a card on the event page and a page of their own listing their sessions. Sponsors appear together, each with a logo, a short description and their tier.

You manage both on the **Event page** tab, under **Page & programme**, in the **Speakers** and **Sponsors** cards under **Section content**.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins, and co-organisers with the **Edit details** permission.

</Callout>

## Adding a speaker

<Steps>

1. Open the event, choose the **Event page** tab, then **Page & programme**.
2. In the **Speakers** card, choose **Add Speaker**.
3. Enter the speaker's **Name**.
4. Choose **Choose speaker photo** and pick a JPEG, PNG or WebP image of up to 5 MB.
5. Fill in any of the optional fields:
   - **Destination URL**: where guests go from the speaker's page, such as their website.
   - **Headline**: a short line such as "Community lead".
   - **Organisation**: where they work or study.
   - **Description**: their bio, which you can format with the text toolbar.
6. Choose a **Visibility**. Only **Published** speakers appear on the public page.
7. Choose **Add Speaker**.

</Steps>

To change a speaker later, choose **Edit** on their row, make your changes and choose **Save changes**. To replace the photo, choose a new one while editing.

### Where speakers appear

- The **Speakers** section of the event page shows a card for each published speaker, with their photo, name, headline and organisation.
- Each card opens the speaker's own page, with their bio, a **Website** button for the destination URL, and a list of their published sessions.
- In the programme, each session lists its speakers. You choose them when you add or edit a session; see [Building the programme](/help/event-page/programme#adding-a-session).

<Callout kind="note">

The form also has **LinkedIn URL** and **X URL** fields. These are saved with the speaker but don't currently appear on the public pages, so put the most useful link in **Destination URL**.

</Callout>

## Adding a sponsor

<Steps>

1. In the **Sponsors** card, choose **Add Sponsor**.
2. Enter the sponsor's **Name**.
3. Choose **Choose sponsor logo** and pick a JPEG, PNG or WebP image of up to 5 MB.
4. Add a **Destination URL** if the logo should link to the sponsor's website.
5. Pick a **Sponsor tier**: **Platinum**, **Gold**, **Silver**, **Partner** or **Community partner**.
6. Add a short **Description** if you like, for example what they're supporting.
7. Choose a **Visibility**, then **Add Sponsor**.

</Steps>

### Where sponsors appear

Published sponsors appear in the event page section titled **Partners**. Each card shows the logo, name, description and tier, and links to the destination URL when there is one. Sponsors appear in the order you added them, with their tier as a label on each card, so add your headline sponsors first.

Sponsors also appear in the [post-event report](/help/after/event-report), grouped by tier.

## Removing a speaker or sponsor

Choose **Remove** on the row and confirm. This can't be undone. Removing a speaker also takes them off every session they were listed on.

If you only want to take someone off the page for now, edit them and set **Visibility** to **Draft** or **Hidden** instead. Their details stay saved for later.

## Good to know

- You need a verified email address to upload photos and logos. If the upload is refused, check your inbox for the verification email; see [Creating an account and signing in](/help/account/sign-up-and-sign-in).
- Uploads are limited for a short while if you upload many images quickly. Wait a moment and try again.
- The Speakers and Sponsors sections only show on the event page when they're switched on in **Sections** and have at least one published entry. See [Building your event page](/help/event-page/building-your-page#sections).
