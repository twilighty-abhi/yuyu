When an event goes well, you'll want to run it again. When plans change, you may need to call it off. This page covers copying an event as a starting point, cancelling one and letting guests know, and deleting an event for good.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins of the organisation. All three live in the event's **Settings** tab.

</Callout>

## Clone an event

Cloning makes a new draft event with the same core information, so you don't start from scratch.

<Steps>

1. Open the event you want to copy and choose the **Settings** tab.
2. In **Run it again**, choose **Clone event**.
3. Yuyu opens the new event's dashboard. Its title ends in *(copy)*.
4. Go to **Event page** → **Details**, update the title, dates and anything else that's different, and save.

</Steps>

The copy is always a **Draft**, so nothing goes public until you publish it. You need a verified email address to clone.

### What's copied

- Title (with *(copy)* added) and cover image
- Start, end and time zone
- Location, map link and whether it's online
- Capacity, who can find it and whether you approve sign-ups
- Team registration settings
- The **Hackathon emails** switches (results emails and submission receipts)
- Whether a guest's No to an [attendance confirmation](/help/guests/attendance-confirmation) frees their place

### What isn't copied

- Guests, crew, invites, check-ins and feedback
- Attendance confirmation questions and answers
- The event page content: tagline, logo, accent colour, sections, highlights, speakers, sponsors, resources, FAQs and the programme
- Registration form questions, emails, the feedback form and certificate
- Co-organisers, tags, the short link, and the sign-up closing rule

<Callout kind="tip">

The copy keeps the original dates, which are often in the past. Set new dates first: the Details form can only be saved once the start time is in the future. You'll also need to fill in **About the event** again before saving.

</Callout>

## Cancel an event

Yuyu doesn't have a separate cancel button. To call off an event, tell your guests, then take the event down:

<Steps>

1. Open the **Emails** tab and start from the **Event cancelled** template. Choose who should get it, check the message and send it. See [Emailing your guests](/help/guests/emailing-guests).
2. Open **Event page** → **Details**, and in **Publishing** choose **Offline**.
3. Choose **Save changes**.

</Steps>

An offline event's page and link stop working and sign-ups close, but the guest list is kept. You can bring it back later by choosing **Live** again.

Send the email first: once you delete an event, you can no longer email its guests.

<Callout kind="note">

Taking an event offline uses the Details form, which can only be saved before the event starts.

</Callout>

## Delete an event

<Callout kind="warning">

Deleting is permanent. The event and all of its registrations, check-ins, feedback and page content are removed, and it can't be undone. Guests aren't emailed.

</Callout>

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Settings** tab.
2. In **Delete event**, choose **Delete event…**.
3. Type the event's address (the last part of its link, shown in **Event address**) to unlock the button.
4. Choose **Delete event**.

</Steps>

Yuyu takes you back to your organisation's dashboard. The event's link and short link stop working.

## Which should I use?

- **Running it again next month or next year?** Clone it, or for a regular meetup, use a [recurring series](/help/events/recurring-series).
- **Called off, but you might reschedule?** Email guests and take it offline. Change the dates and make it live again when you're ready.
- **Created by mistake, or no longer needed at all?** Delete it.
- **Finished normally?** Leave it. Past events stay on your dashboard under **Past**, with their analytics and report.
