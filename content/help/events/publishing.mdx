Publishing decides three things: whether your event is visible at all, who can find it and sign up, and how many places there are and for how long. You set all three in one place, the **Publishing** card at the bottom of **Event page** → **Details**.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins. Co-organisers can't publish an event or change these settings, even with **Publish and schedule**, which covers the programme.

</Callout>

## Publish your event

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose **Event page** → **Details**.
2. Scroll to **Publishing**.
3. Under **Who can see this event?**, choose **Live**.
4. Check **Who can find it?** and **Sign-ups** (see below).
5. Choose **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom.

</Steps>

The line at the top of the card sums up what guests experience, for example: *Live. Listed publicly, anyone can sign up · 120 spots · closes 2 hours before.*

If you're happy with the settings already, the quickest way is **Publish event** in the **Overview** tab's checklist. It makes the event live without changing who can find it or the sign-up rules.

## Who can see it

| Choice | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Only your team can see it. Nobody can sign up yet. Every new event starts here. |
| **Live** | The event page is public and people can sign up. |
| **Offline** | Taken down. The link stops working and sign-ups close, but guests keep their places. |

While an event is a draft or offline, owners, admins and co-organisers with **Edit details** or **Publish and schedule** can still open the page to preview it. They see a *Preview mode* note at the top.

In the dashboard header and the **At a glance** card, a live event shows as **Published** and an offline one as **Hidden**.

## Who can find it

These choices take effect once the event is live.

- **Everyone**: listed on your organisation's public page, on Discover and in search. Search engines can find it too.
- **People with the link**: not listed anywhere. Anyone you share the link with can open it and sign up.
- **Invited people only**: only people you invite by email can open the page. They need to sign in with the invited address. See [Invite-only events](/help/registration/invitations).

### Approve each sign-up

Turn on **Approve each sign-up** if you want to choose who gets a place. People ask to join and wait until you accept them in **Guests**. With it off, people get their place straight away.

You can combine it with **Everyone** (people find the event publicly, then ask to join) or **People with the link**. It isn't needed for invite-only events: invited people are confirmed when they sign up. See [Capacity, waitlists and approvals](/help/registration/capacity-and-waitlist).

## Sign-ups

### Limit the number of spots

Turn on **Limit the number of spots** and enter a number. When it's full, new sign-ups join the waitlist. With it off, everyone who signs up gets a place.

You can't set a limit lower than the number of people who already have a place. On team events, each team counts as one place, and so does each person signing up on their own.

### Close sign-ups

Pick one rule. After it, the sign-up button turns off.

- **At the end**: sign-ups stay open until the event ends.
- **Before start**: close a set number of minutes, hours or days before the start. This moves with the start time if you change it.
- **Set time**: close at a date and time you choose, on or before the start, in the event's time zone.

A line underneath explains exactly when sign-ups close.

### Team registration

Turn this on for hackathons and competitions where people sign up in teams. It can't be switched on or off once anyone has registered. See [Setting up team registration](/help/registration/team-registration).

### Show how many are going

With **Show how many are going** on, guests see the number of sign-ups on the event page.

## Good to know

- The Details form can only be saved while the event's start time is in the future, so make publishing changes before the event starts.
- A short link only works while the event is live. See [Sharing your event](/help/events/sharing).
- Changing who can find the event doesn't remove anyone who already signed up.
