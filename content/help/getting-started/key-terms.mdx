Yuyu uses a small set of words consistently across the app, its emails and this guide. If a screen uses a word you don't recognise, it is probably explained here.

## Organisations and people

- **Organisation**: the group that runs events, such as your club, meetup, society or volunteer team. Every event and series belongs to one organisation. See [Creating an organisation](/help/organisations/create).
- **Member**: someone who belongs to an organisation. Each member has one role:

| Role | In short |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything an admin can do, plus decisions kept for owners, such as deleting the organisation. Every organisation has at least one owner. |
| **Admin** | Creates, edits, publishes and runs the organisation's events. |
| **Member** | Can open the organisation's event dashboards to look around, and changes an event only with co-organiser permissions on it. |

- **Co-organiser**: a person given exact permissions on one event or series (for example checking people in, or managing registrations) without joining the organisation. See [Event co-organisers](/help/organisations/co-organisers).
- **Guest** or **attendee**: anyone who registers for an event. Guests don't need a Yuyu account.

[Roles and permissions](/help/organisations/roles) has the full detail.

## Events

- **Event**: a single gathering with a date, time, place or online link, and its own guest list.
- **Recurring series**: a repeating event, such as a weekly meetup. Each date in a series is an **occurrence** with its own guest list. See [Recurring series](/help/events/recurring-series).
- **Event page**: the public web page for an event, with its details, programme, speakers, sponsors and the registration button.
- **Event dashboard**: where organisers run one event, in tabs: **Overview**, **Guests**, **Event page**, **Emails**, **Check-in**, **Feedback**, **Analytics** and **Settings**. Hackathons also get a **Judging** tab.
- **Draft**, **Live** and **Offline**: an event's publishing state. Draft is visible only to your team. Live means the event page is public and people can sign up. Offline takes the page down, and guests keep their places. See [Publishing and visibility](/help/events/publishing).
- **Short link**: an optional short address for an event that is easy to print or say out loud. It only works while the event is Live.

## Registration

- **Registration**, **RSVP** or **sign-up**: one person's place at an event or occurrence. Each registration has a status:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** (organisers see **Going**) | The person has a place and a scannable ticket. |
| **Waitlisted** | The event was full. They can move up if a place frees up. |
| **Waiting for approval** (organisers see **To approve**) | The organisers review each sign-up before confirming it. |
| **Declined** | The organisers turned the registration down. |
| **Checked in** | The guest has arrived and was checked in at the door. |

- **Capacity** or **spots**: the most confirmed places an event allows. When it is full, new sign-ups join the waitlist. See [Capacity, waitlists and approvals](/help/registration/capacity-and-waitlist).
- **Registration form**: the questions people answer when they register. Name and email are always asked; organisers can add more. See [The registration form](/help/registration/form).
- **Invite-only**: an event that only people on its invite list can open and register for. See [Invite-only events](/help/registration/invitations).

## On the day

- **Ticket**: a guest's personal page with a QR code, linked from their confirmation email. It is unique to one registration. See [Your ticket](/help/attending/your-ticket).
- **Check-in**: marking a guest as arrived, by scanning their QR code, pasting their ticket link or searching their name. See [The check-in desk](/help/check-in/check-in-desk).
- **Door station**: a PIN-protected check-in page for volunteers who don't have a Yuyu account. See [The door station](/help/check-in/door-station).
- **Check-in list**: an extra list alongside entry, for things like swag, meals or a workshop. See [Custom check-in lists](/help/check-in/check-in-lists).

## Teams and hackathons

- **Team event**: an event where people register in teams. Each team, and each person registering on their own, takes **one place** towards capacity, however many members the team has.
- **Team leader**: the person who created the team. They manage it from a private **team page**.
- **Join link**: the link a team leader shares so teammates can register straight into the team. See [Registering as a team](/help/attending/teams).
- **Entrant** and **project**: in a hackathon, an entrant is a team, or one person taking part on their own. Each entrant can submit one project for judging. See [Submitting a hackathon project](/help/attending/submitting-a-project).

## After the event

- **Feedback form**: a short survey the organisers open after the event. It is anonymous unless the event offers certificates.
- **Certificate**: a certificate of participation that guests download after giving feedback, when the organisers turn certificates on. See [Feedback and certificates](/help/attending/feedback-and-certificates).
