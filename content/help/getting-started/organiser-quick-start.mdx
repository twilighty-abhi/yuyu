This walkthrough takes you from a brand-new account to a published event with guests checked in at the door. Each step links to a fuller article when you want more detail.

## 1. Create your account

<Steps>

1. Choose **Sign in** in the site header, open the **Create account** tab, and enter your name, email and a password of at least 12 characters.
2. Open the verification email and choose **Confirm email** on the page it opens.
3. Sign in.

</Steps>

You must confirm your email before you can create events. See [Creating an account and signing in](/help/account/sign-up-and-sign-in) for Google sign-in and other details.

## 2. Create your organisation

Everything you run in Yuyu belongs to an organisation. Your dashboard shows a short setup checklist that starts here.

<Steps>

1. On your dashboard, choose **Create organisation**.
2. Enter a **Name** and a **Web address**. The web address becomes your organisation's public page, so keep it short: lowercase letters, numbers and dashes.
3. Optionally add a **Description** and a **Logo**, then choose **Create organisation**.

</Steps>

You become the organisation's owner. See [Creating an organisation](/help/organisations/create).

## 3. Create the event

<Steps>

1. In your organisation's dashboard, choose **New event**.
2. **Basics**: give the event a **Title** and fill in **About the event**. Tags are optional.
3. **Schedule**: set the **Start**, **End** and **Timezone**. The start must be in the future.
4. **Location & sign-ups**: enter the venue in **Location**, or turn on **Online event** and paste the meeting link. You can add a **Map link** for in-person events.
5. Choose **Create**.

</Steps>

Yuyu creates the event as a **Draft** and opens its event dashboard. Nobody else can see it yet, so take your time. See [Creating an event](/help/events/create).

## 4. Make it look right

Open the **Event page** tab to add a cover photo, check the details, and decide what you ask people when they register.

- **Details** holds the cover image, schedule, location and the **Publishing** settings.
- **Page & programme** builds the public page: highlights, sessions, speakers, sponsors and FAQs.
- **Registration form** adds questions beyond name and email.

The **Overview** tab shows a readiness checklist (for example "No cover image" or "No programme yet") with a shortcut to fix each item.

## 5. Publish and share

New events are open to everyone with unlimited places. To change that before you publish, open **Event page** → **Details** → **Publishing**: you can make the event findable only by link or by invitation, ask to **Approve each sign-up**, and **Limit the number of spots**. See [Publishing and visibility](/help/events/publishing) and [Capacity, waitlists and approvals](/help/registration/capacity-and-waitlist).

When you're ready:

<Steps>

1. On the **Overview** tab, find "Not published yet" in the checklist and choose **Publish event**. (You can also choose **Live** under **Who can see this event?** in the Publishing section and save.)
2. Copy the event's address and share it. You can also create a short link from the **Overview** tab.

</Steps>

<Callout kind="permission">

Only organisation owners and admins can create and publish events.

</Callout>

See [Sharing your event](/help/events/sharing) for short links, social cards and the embeddable card.

## 6. Look after your guests

As people register, they appear in the **Guests** tab. From there you can approve or decline sign-ups, give waitlisted people a place, add guests by hand, and export the list. The **Emails** tab sends reminders and updates to the people coming.

- [The guest list](/help/guests/guest-list)
- [Emailing your guests](/help/guests/emailing-guests)

Every guest with a confirmed place gets an email with a link to their ticket and its QR code.

## 7. Check people in

<Steps>

1. On the day, open the **Check-in** tab and choose **Open check-in** on the phone, tablet or laptop you'll use at the door.
2. Scan each guest's QR code, paste their ticket link, or search by name.
3. If the venue Wi-Fi is unreliable, choose **Download guest list for offline** beforehand so the desk keeps working without a connection.

</Steps>

Volunteers without a Yuyu account can use the PIN-locked [door station](/help/check-in/door-station) instead. See [The check-in desk](/help/check-in/check-in-desk).

## 8. Follow up

After the event, open a [feedback form](/help/after/feedback), optionally with [certificates](/help/after/certificates), and see how it went in [Analytics](/help/after/analytics). Owners and admins can download a [post-event report](/help/after/event-report) once the event has ended.
