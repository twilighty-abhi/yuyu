Yuyu is a home for free community events. Clubs, meetups, campus groups, NGOs and volunteer teams use it to publish an event page, take registrations, email guests, check people in at the door, and follow up afterwards with feedback and certificates.

Every event on Yuyu is free to attend. Yuyu has no ticket sales, payments, ads or trackers.

## Who this guide is for

The guide is written for everyone who touches Yuyu, so start with the part that fits you:

- **Going to an event?** Read [Going to events](/help/attending/registering). It covers registering, your ticket, teams, and certificates.
- **Running events?** Start with [Run your first event](/help/getting-started/organiser-quick-start), then dip into the sections on creating events, registration, guests and check-in as you need them.
- **Looking after your organisation?** [Organisations and people](/help/organisations/roles) explains roles, members and co-organisers.
- **Running this Yuyu server?** If you are the instance's super-admin, an extra *Instance administration* section appears in the sidebar once you sign in.

## How Yuyu fits together

Everything in Yuyu belongs to an **organisation**: your club, group or team. An organisation has members, and each member has a role that decides what they can change.

Organisations run **events**. Each event has its own public page, registration form, guest list, emails, check-in desk and feedback form. A meetup that repeats every week can be a **recurring series**, where every date has its own guest list.

When someone registers, they get a **ticket** with a QR code. At the door, you scan it from the check-in desk on any phone or tablet, even without Wi-Fi.

```text
Organisation
 ├── Members (owners, admins, members)
 ├── Events ── page · registration form · guests · emails · check-in · feedback
 └── Recurring series ── one guest list per date
```

## Finding your way around

| Where | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Discover** | Browse public events on this Yuyu instance. |
| **Dashboard** | Your organisations, their events and series. Organisers spend most of their time here. |
| **Event dashboard** | Everything about one event, in tabs: Overview, Guests, Event page, Emails, Check-in, Feedback, Analytics and Settings. |
| **Account** | Your profile, registrations and tickets, organisations, and security settings. |

## A note on permissions

Some features are only available to certain roles, for example only owners and admins can publish an event. When a page covers something not everyone can do, you'll see a **Who can do this** box like this one:

<Callout kind="permission">
Owners and admins of the organisation. Co-organisers can also do this if they were given the matching permission for the event.
</Callout>

If a button described in the guide is missing for you, you probably don't have that permission. Ask an owner or admin of your organisation.
