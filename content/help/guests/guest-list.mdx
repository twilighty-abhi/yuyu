The guest list shows everyone who has registered for your event, what state their registration is in, and whether they've checked in. It's where you approve requests, give waitlisted guests a place, add people yourself and fix mistakes.

## Opening the guest list

Open the event and choose the **Guests** tab, then **People**. Each row shows the guest's name and email, when they registered, their status and their check-in time ("Not yet" for guests going who haven't arrived).

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins, and co-organisers with the **Registrations** permission. Undoing a check-in also needs the **Check-in** permission.

</Callout>

<Screenshot name="guests-people" alt="The Guests tab People list with status filters, search and guest rows" />

The list loads 50 guests at a time; choose **Load more guests** at the bottom for the next batch. The counts on the filters always cover the whole event.

## Guest statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Going** | Has a place and a ticket. |
| **Waitlist** | Signed up when the event was full and is waiting for a place. |
| **To approve** | Asked to join an event where you approve each sign-up. |
| **Declined** | You turned down their request or removed them from the waitlist. |

Use the filter chips across the top (**All**, **Going**, **Waitlist**, **To approve**, **Declined**, **Checked in**, **Not checked in**) to narrow the list. Next to them you can show **Everyone**, **Guests only** (people who registered without an account) or **Signed-in users**, and search by name or email. On team events you can also filter by **In a team** or **Solo**, and search by team name.

To keep a filter you use often, save it as a view. See [Filters and saved views](/help/guests/saved-views).

## Approving, declining and giving places

The buttons on each row change with the guest's status:

- **To approve**: **Approve** gives them a place; **Decline** turns them down.
- **Waitlist**: **Give a spot** gives them a place if one is free. To turn them down, open the row's menu and choose **Decline**.
- **Declined**: **Re-accept** gives them a place if there's room, or puts them on the waitlist if the event is full.

Each change emails the guest: a ticket when they get a place, or a note when they're declined. See [Automatic emails](/help/guests/automatic-emails).

If **Approve** or **Give a spot** says the event is at capacity, raise the place limit or free a place first. See [Capacity, waitlists and approvals](/help/registration/capacity-and-waitlist).

On team events, people in a team get a place or are declined with their whole team. Their row shows **Manage in Teams** instead; see [Managing teams](/help/guests/managing-teams).

## Reading answers

If your registration form has questions, choose **Answers** on a row to show what that guest wrote. The number next to it is how many questions they answered.

## Adding a guest yourself

Add someone who registered by phone, a speaker, or a volunteer.

<Steps>

1. On the **People** sub-tab, choose **Add guest**.
2. Enter their **Name** and **Email**, and answer any registration questions.
3. Choose **Add attendee**.

</Steps>

They're emailed their ticket straight away. The usual rules still apply: they join the waitlist if the event is full, or wait under **To approve** if you approve each sign-up. You can add guests even after sign-ups have closed. An email address can only be registered once per event.

## Editing a registration or transferring a ticket

Open the row's menu and choose **Edit registration or transfer ticket**. You can correct answers, or change the name and email to hand the ticket to someone else, then choose **Save transfer**. The ticket link, status and check-in stay the same, and no email is sent, so use **Copy ticket link** from the menu to send the new person their ticket.

For people who registered with a Yuyu account, the menu says **View registration** instead. You can update their answers but not move their ticket to someone else.

## Removing a guest

Open the row's menu, choose **Remove RSVP** and confirm. A message appears with **Undo** for about ten seconds; choose it to bring the registration back exactly as it was.

<Callout kind="warning">

Removing a guest doesn't email them, and it doesn't give their place to the next person on the waitlist. If the event was full, give the free place to someone with **Give a spot**.

</Callout>

## Other actions

- **Copy ticket link** copies the guest's private ticket link. Treat it like a ticket: anyone with the link can use it.
- **Undo check-in** marks a checked-in guest as not checked in, after you confirm.
- On team events, **Add to a team** or **Move to another team** puts a guest into a team with room.

## Good to know

- Guests who cancel their own registration disappear from the list. If they had a place, the person who has waited longest on the waitlist gets it automatically, unless you approve each sign-up.
- Chips under a guest show the custom check-in lists they've been checked into, such as meals or swag. See [Custom check-in lists](/help/check-in/check-in-lists).
- To download the list, see [Exporting guest data](/help/guests/exports).
