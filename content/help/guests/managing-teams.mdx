On an event with team registration, the **Teams** sub-tab of the **Guests** tab lists every team with its members. Places are given out team by team, so this is where you approve, decline and promote teams, and tidy up names and members.

The Teams sub-tab only appears when team registration is on. To set it up, see [Setting up team registration](/help/registration/team-registration).

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins, and co-organisers with the **Registrations** permission.

</Callout>

## Reading the Teams list

Each team shows its name, size (for example "4 people · up to 5"), how many members have checked in, and its status: **Going**, **Waitlist**, **To approve** or **Declined**. Two extra labels may appear:

- **Incomplete**: the team is smaller than your minimum team size.
- **Looking for members**: the leader has asked for more people to join.

Below each team you see its members, with a **Leader** label on the person who created or now runs it.

Use the filter chips (**All**, **Going**, **Waitlist**, **To approve**, **Declined**, **Incomplete**, **Looking for members**) and **Search team or member** to find a team. The list loads in batches; choose **Load more teams** for the next one.

## Approving, declining and giving places

A team's members always share the team's status, so you act on the whole team at once:

- **To approve**: choose **Approve** to give every member a place, or **Decline** to turn the team down.
- **Waitlist**: choose **Give a place** to admit the team when there's a free place, or **Decline team** from the team's menu.

Every member is emailed when their team gets a place or is declined. Declining asks you to confirm, because everyone in the team will be told they don't have a place.

<Callout kind="note">

You can't approve an **Incomplete** team. The row explains how many people it needs; wait for it to grow, or move people into it.

</Callout>

On the **People** sub-tab, a team member's row shows **Manage in Teams** instead of approve or decline buttons, because individual team members can't be approved or declined on their own.

## Renaming a team

Open the team's menu, choose **Rename team**, enter the new **Team name** and choose **Save**. Team names must be unique within the event.

## Moving someone to another team

<Steps>

1. Open the menu next to the member and choose **Move to another team**.
2. Search for and pick the team they should join. Only teams with room are listed.
3. Choose **Move**.

</Steps>

The person takes the new team's status. For example, moving someone into a team that's going gives them a place and emails their ticket. A person who has already checked in can only move to a team that's going, and you can't move anyone into a declined team.

You can do the same from the **People** sub-tab: open a guest's menu and choose **Move to another team**, or **Add to a team** for someone registered on their own.

If moving someone leaves their old team empty, that team is removed.

## Removing a member

Open the menu next to the member, choose **Remove from event** and confirm. Their registration is cancelled. If they were the leader, leadership passes to the longest-standing member; if they were the only member, the team is removed too.

## Deleting a team

Open the team's menu, choose **Delete team** and confirm. The team and all its members' registrations are removed.

Both removing a member and deleting a team show an **Undo** button for about ten seconds, which restores everything as it was.

<Callout kind="warning">

Deleting a team doesn't email its members. If the team had a place, the oldest team or solo registrant on the waitlist gets it automatically, unless you approve each sign-up.

</Callout>

## Good to know

- On team events, a place limit counts teams and solo registrants, not people. See [Capacity, waitlists and approvals](/help/registration/capacity-and-waitlist).
- **Export CSV** on the Teams sub-tab downloads every team matching your filter and search. See [Exporting guest data](/help/guests/exports).
- The **Views** menu has presets for **Incomplete**, **Pending approval** and **Looking for members** teams. See [Filters and saved views](/help/guests/saved-views).
- Leaders manage their own team from their team page. For what they can do, see [Registering as a team](/help/attending/teams).
