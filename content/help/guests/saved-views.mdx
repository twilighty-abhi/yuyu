Long guest lists are easier to work with when you can jump straight to the people you need: everyone on the waitlist, guests who haven't arrived yet, or teams still waiting for approval. Filters narrow the list, and saved views let you keep a set of filters and come back to it in one click, or share it with the rest of your team.

## Filters and the address bar

On the **Guests** tab, the **People** and **Teams** sub-tabs each have filter chips, a search box and, on People, drop-downs for the kind of registration and team. For what each filter does, see [The guest list](/help/guests/guest-list#guest-statuses) and [Managing teams](/help/guests/managing-teams#reading-the-teams-list).

The filters you pick are kept in the page address. That means:

- Reloading the page keeps your filters.
- A bookmark or a link you paste to a teammate opens the same filtered list.
- Switching to another tab or sub-tab clears them.

<Callout kind="note">

A shared link or view never gives anyone extra access. People who can't normally see the guest list still can't see it, whatever link they open.

</Callout>

## The Views menu

Each list has a **Views** button next to the export buttons. When a view is applied, the button shows its name, for example **Views · Waitlisted**.

<Screenshot name="guests-views-menu" alt="The Views menu on the Guests People list, showing presets, your views and shared views" />

The menu has up to four groups:

- **Presets**: built-in views that are always there.
- **Your views**: views you saved. A **Shared** label shows the ones your team can see.
- **Shared views**: views teammates saved and shared, with the name of the person who made each one.
- Actions: **Save current view…**, **Manage views…**, **Copy link**, and **Clear filters** once a filter is applied.

### Presets

| List | Presets |
| --- | --- |
| **People** | **All**, **Waitlisted**, **Checked in**, **Not checked in** (going but not yet at the door) and, on team events, **Solo** |
| **Teams** | **All**, **Incomplete**, **Pending approval**, **Looking for members** |

## Saving a view

<Steps>

1. Set the filters and search you want on **People** or **Teams**.
2. Open **Views** and choose **Save current view…**.
3. Give the view a **Name** of up to 60 characters.
4. Tick **Share with the team** if everyone who can open this list on this event should see it.
5. Choose **Save view**.

</Steps>

**Save current view…** is greyed out until you've picked at least one filter or typed a search.

Views belong to one list on one event. A view saved on **People** doesn't appear on **Teams**, and views don't carry over to other events.

<Callout kind="permission">

Anyone who can see the list can save, apply and share views: owners and admins, and co-organisers with the **Registrations** permission. Owners and admins can also delete views other people shared.

</Callout>

## Managing your views

Open **Views** and choose **Manage views…**. For each view you made, you can:

- Turn **Share** on or off.
- Rename it with the pencil button.
- Delete it with the bin button, after you confirm.

Views other people shared appear in the list too, marked with who made them. Only the person who made a view can rename or re-share it.

## Copying a link

Choose **Copy link** to copy a link to the list with its current filters. It's the quickest way to point a teammate at, say, the 12 people still waiting for approval, without saving a view.

## Good to know

- Each person can keep up to 50 views per list. A menu shows up to 100 views, your own first.
- Views store filters, not people. A view always shows the current list, so "Waitlisted" changes as people join or leave the waitlist.
- Exports use whatever filters are showing. Apply a view, then choose **Export CSV** to download just those guests. See [Exporting guest data](/help/guests/exports).
- The judging lists for hackathons have their own views and presets. See [Assigning judges and tracking progress](/help/hackathons/progress).
