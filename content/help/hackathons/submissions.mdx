Once submissions are on, entrants send in their projects during the window you set. The **Submissions** section of the **Judging** tab is where you watch them arrive, check who hasn't submitted yet, and deal with any that break the rules. After the window closes, you can also show every project in a public gallery.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins of the organisation. Judges see projects in their own scoring workspace instead. See [Judges, rubric and scoring](/help/hackathons/judging).

</Callout>

## The submission window

Set the window in **Judging** → **Setup**, in the **Project submissions** card:

- **Opens** and **Closes** are in the event's time zone. Yuyu checks the time every time an entrant saves, so nobody can sneak a change in after the deadline.
- Choose **Save settings** after changing them.

Only confirmed entrants can submit. On a team event, that's a confirmed team. On an individual event, or for a solo registrant at a team event, it's a confirmed registration. Entrants reach the form from their ticket or team page while the event is live.

A project has a title (up to 120 characters), a plain-text summary (up to 4,000 characters), and optional links to the code, a demo and a video. Entrants can save a draft, submit, edit while the window is open, and withdraw. Whoever submits gets a confirmation email. The entrants' side is covered in [Submitting a hackathon project](/help/attending/submitting-a-project).

<Callout kind="note">

If a team is disbanded or a solo registration is cancelled, its project goes with it. If you delete a team or registration by mistake and undo it, the project and its scores come back too.

</Callout>

## Reviewing submissions

Open **Judging** → **Submissions** to see every project, oldest first.

<Screenshot name="judging-submissions" alt="The Submissions section with status filters, search, and a list of projects with their status" />

- Filter by status with **All**, **Submitted**, **Drafts**, **Withdrawn**, **Disqualified** and **Not submitted**. Each shows a count.
- Type in **Search projects** to find a project by title, team name or registrant name.
- Choose **Load more** to see more projects.
- Use the **Views** menu to save filters you use often. See [Filters and saved views](/help/guests/saved-views).

Only **Submitted** projects are judged. Drafts and withdrawn projects stay visible here but never reach the judges.

**Not submitted** lists the confirmed teams and solo entrants who don't have a submitted project yet, whether they have nothing, a draft, or a withdrawn or disqualified project. Use it to send a reminder before the deadline. See [Emailing your guests](/help/guests/emailing-guests).

Choose the **⋯** button on a project for its actions:

- **View project** shows the title, summary and links.
- **Judges' reviews** shows each judge's scores and their private comment.
- **Assign judges** picks who reviews it, when judges only see assigned projects. See [Assigning judges and tracking progress](/help/hackathons/progress).
- **Disqualify…** and **Reinstate…** are described below.

## Disqualifying a project

<Steps>

1. Choose **⋯** on the project, then **Disqualify…**.
2. Enter a **Reason**. Only organisers see it; the entrant isn't told.
3. Read the warning, then choose **Disqualify**.

</Steps>

A disqualified project stops being judged and drops out of the ranking. If it holds any awards, it loses them, and if results are already published the live results page changes straight away.

To undo this, choose **Reinstate…**. The project goes back into judging and the ranking, but it doesn't get its awards back. Assign them again on the **Awards** tab if it should still win.

## Exporting submissions

Choose **Export CSV** to download exactly the projects matching your filter and search. The file includes each project's entrant, whether it's a team or solo entry, its status, submitted and updated times in the event's time zone, the summary, links, assigned judges and any disqualification reason. With **Not submitted** selected, it lists those entrants instead.

## The public project gallery

To show off everyone's work, turn on **Public project gallery** in **Setup** and choose **Save settings**. Once the submission window closes, the event's projects page lists every submitted project, 24 to a page.

- Visitors can search by project title or team name, and sort by **Newest** or **A–Z**.
- After you publish results, award winners get badges, and visitors can filter to **Award winners** or sort by **Awards first**. Winners are never revealed before that.
- The gallery follows the event page's visibility. If the event is private, invite-only or offline, so is the gallery.

## Good to know

- Moving the closing time is how you stop new projects. You can't turn submissions off once judging has started.
- While submissions are on and the event is a draft, offline or cancelled, the **Project submissions** card warns that entrants can't submit.
