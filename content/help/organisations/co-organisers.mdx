A co-organiser is someone who helps with one event without joining your organisation. You choose exactly what they can do: a door volunteer might only get **Check-in**, while a partner running sign-ups gets **Registrations**. They see only the events they're invited to, never your other events, members or settings.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins of the organisation invite, change and remove co-organisers.

</Callout>

## Invite a co-organiser

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Settings** tab.
2. In the **Co-organisers** card, under **Invite someone**, enter their email address.
3. Choose what they can do by turning permissions on or off. **Edit details** is on to start with; pick at least one.
4. Choose **Send invite**.

</Steps>

Yuyu emails them a link to accept. The invite shows in the list as **Invited**, with how long it has left. Invites expire after 7 days.

To accept, the person opens the link, signs in (or creates an account) **with the same email address you invited**, and chooses **Accept invite**. They land on the event's dashboard. If they sign in with a different address, the invite shows as unavailable.

## The permissions

| Permission | What it lets them do |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit details** | Edit the event page content in **Event page** → **Page & programme**: colour, sections, highlights, speakers, sponsors, resources and FAQs. Set up custom check-in lists. Preview the page while it's a draft. |
| **Registrations** | Work the guest list in **Guests**: approve, decline, give waitlist places, add, edit and remove guests, and export CSVs. See the registration numbers and **Analytics**. |
| **Attendee invites** | Manage the invite list in **Guests** → **Invites**. |
| **Check-in** | Open the check-in desk: scan tickets, search for guests, check people in and out of lists, work offline and print badges. |
| **Publish and schedule** | Build the programme: sessions, tracks and live delays. Preview the page while it's a draft. |
| **Judge** | Score hackathon projects in the judging workspace. See [Judges, rubric and scoring](/help/hackathons/judging). |

Permissions are exact: one never includes another. Someone with only **Check-in** can't see the guest list in **Guests**, and someone with only **Registrations** can't open the check-in desk.

<Callout kind="note" title="Some things stay with owners and admins">

Whatever permissions they have, co-organisers can't edit the **Details** form (title, dates, location, cover and publishing), make the event live, change the registration form, send emails, manage feedback and certificates, download reports, or use the **Settings** tab. Despite its name, **Publish and schedule** covers the programme, not making the event live.

</Callout>

A co-organiser whose only permission is **Judge** sees just the judging workspace for that event, and nothing else on the dashboard.

## Change or remove access

In the **Co-organisers** card, each person who has accepted shows as **Active** with their permissions.

- To change what they can do, choose **Edit**, turn permissions on or off, and choose **Save permissions**.
- To take away their access, choose **Remove**, then **Remove access**. They lose access to the event straight away.
- To withdraw an invite that hasn't been accepted, choose **Cancel invite**. The link in their email stops working.

## What co-organisers see

When a co-organiser opens your organisation in Yuyu, they see only a list of the events they help with. Each event's dashboard shows the tabs and buttons their permissions allow, with a short note where they don't have permission.

Their co-organiser roles, and any invites waiting for them, are also listed in **Account** → **Organisations**.

## Good to know

- To re-invite someone whose invite expired, send a new invite from the same card.
- If an organisation member also co-organises an event, they get the co-organiser permissions on that event on top of their member role.
- Removing someone from your organisation doesn't remove their co-organiser access. Remove it here as well if needed.
- See [Roles and permissions](/help/organisations/roles) for how co-organisers compare with members and admins.
