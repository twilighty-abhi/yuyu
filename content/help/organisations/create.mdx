An organisation is the home for your club, group or team on Yuyu. Every event, recurring series, member and setting belongs to one. Create one when you're ready to run your first event, or when a new group needs its own public page and team.

Anyone with a Yuyu account can create an organisation, and you can belong to as many as you like.

## Create your organisation

<Steps>

1. Choose **New organisation** in the sidebar. If you don't belong to an organisation yet, you can also choose **Create organisation** on your dashboard home.
2. Enter a **Name**. Guests see it on your public page and at the top of every event page.
3. Enter a **Web address**. Yuyu checks it as you type and tells you whether it's available.
4. Add a **Description** if you like: a sentence or two about your community.
5. Add a **Logo** if you have one: a JPEG, PNG or WebP image up to 5 MB.
6. Choose **Create organisation**.

</Steps>

Yuyu opens your organisation's public page. Choose **Dashboard** at the top to start setting things up.

## Choosing a web address

The web address is the last part of your public page's link, for example `/riverside-coders`. It's also the start of every event link, such as `/riverside-coders/spring-meetup`.

- Use lowercase letters, numbers and single hyphens. Spaces turn into hyphens as you type.
- It must be 2 to 64 characters long.
- Some words are reserved for Yuyu's own pages, such as `dashboard`, `discover`, `help` and `settings`, so they show as taken.

<Callout kind="warning">

You can't change the web address after the organisation is created, so pick one you're happy to keep. The name, description and logo can all be changed later in [Organisation settings](/help/organisations/settings).

</Callout>

## You're the owner

The person who creates an organisation becomes its **Owner**. The owner can do everything, including changing other people's roles, managing API access and deleting the organisation. See [Roles and permissions](/help/organisations/roles) for what each role can do.

An organisation has one owner, and ownership can't be handed to someone else in the app. If someone else should run things day to day, make them an admin.

## What to do next

A brand-new organisation's dashboard shows a short checklist:

- **Add your logo and a short description.** They appear on your public page and every event page.
- **Create your first event**, or start a series for a weekly or monthly meetup. See [Creating an event](/help/events/create) and [Recurring series](/help/events/recurring-series).
- **Invite your team.** See [Inviting and managing members](/help/organisations/members).
- **Share your public page.** It's one link that always lists what's coming up.

## Good to know

- You need a verified email address to create events and series. Creating the organisation itself doesn't need it, but you'll be asked to verify before your first event.
- If Yuyu says there have been too many creation attempts, wait a while and try again.
- Your public page lists only events that are live and open to everyone. Drafts, link-only and invite-only events never appear there. See [Publishing and visibility](/help/events/publishing).
