Organisation settings control how your organisation looks to guests: its name, description and logo, which show on your public page and at the top of every event page. Owners also manage API access and can delete the organisation from here.

Open **Settings** in the sidebar, or from the **Settings** button on your organisation's dashboard.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins. **API access** and **Delete organisation** are for the owner only.

</Callout>

## Edit your profile

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings**.
2. In the **Profile** card, change the **Logo**, **Name** or **Description**.
3. Check the **Preview · public page** beside the form. It updates as you type.
4. Choose **Save changes**.

</Steps>

Nothing changes for guests until you save. **Discard** puts back what was saved. If you try to leave the page with unsaved changes, Yuyu asks you first.

- **Logo**: a JPEG, PNG or WebP image up to 5 MB.
- **Name**: up to 120 characters.
- **Description**: up to 2,000 characters, shown under your name on the public page.

## Your public page

The **Public page** card shows your organisation's address, such as `/riverside-coders`. This one link always lists your upcoming events that are live and open to everyone.

- **Copy link** copies the full address so you can share it.
- **Open** takes you to the page.

The address is the web address you chose when you created the organisation. It can't be changed.

## Connecting other tools

Two more cards connect Yuyu to your own systems:

- **Webhooks** (owners and admins) send registrations, check-ins and other activity to another tool as it happens. Choose **Manage webhooks**.
- **API access** (owner only) lets another system read your events with a key. Choose **Manage API access**.

Both are covered in the *Connecting other tools* section of this guide, which owners and admins can see in the sidebar.

## Delete the organisation

<Callout kind="permission">

Only the owner.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="warning">

Deleting an organisation permanently removes it with all of its events, recurring series, registrations and memberships. It can't be undone, and guests aren't told.

</Callout>

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** and scroll to **Delete organisation**.
2. Choose **Delete organisation…**.
3. Type the organisation's exact name to unlock the button.
4. Choose **Delete**.

</Steps>

For security, Yuyu only allows this within 10 minutes of signing in. If you see "For security, sign in again before deleting an organisation", sign out, sign back in and try again.

If you have upcoming events, email your guests first from each event's **Emails** tab. See [Emailing your guests](/help/guests/emailing-guests).

## Good to know

- Members and co-organisers can't open organisation settings. If you can't see **Settings** in the sidebar, ask an owner or admin.
- Each event can also have its own logo and accent colour on its event page. See [Event details](/help/events/details).
- If you only want to step back yourself, you don't need to delete anything: leave from **Account** → **Organisations**. See [Your data and memberships](/help/account/your-data).
