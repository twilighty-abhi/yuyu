Use these settings to control how places are given out: cap the number of places, let a waitlist form when you're full, approve each person yourself, and choose when sign-ups close.

## Where these settings live

They're all in the **Publishing** card on the **Event page** tab, under **Details**. Change what you need, then choose **Save changes** in the bar that appears at the bottom of the screen.

<Callout kind="permission">

Organisation owners and admins. Co-organisers with the **Registrations** permission can then approve, decline and give places from the **Guests** tab.

</Callout>

## Limiting places

<Steps>

1. In **Publishing**, under **Sign-ups**, turn on **Limit the number of spots**.
2. Enter the number of places.
3. Choose **Save changes**.

</Steps>

With the switch off, places are unlimited and everyone who signs up gets one. On a team event, each team counts as one place, and so does each person signing up on their own; see [Setting up team registration](/help/registration/team-registration).

You can raise the limit at any time. You can't lower it below the number of people who already have a place.

## The waitlist

When every place is taken, the register button on the event page changes to **Join the waitlist**. People who sign up then are put on the waitlist and get an email saying so. They appear under **Waitlist** on the **Guests** tab.

People leave the waitlist in three ways:

- **Automatically.** When someone with a place cancels their own registration, the person who has waited longest gets the place and is emailed their ticket.
- **When you give them a place.** Choose **Give a spot** on their row in the **Guests** tab. This only works while there's a free place, so raise the limit first if the event is full.
- **When you decline them.** Choose **Decline** from the row's menu.

<Callout kind="note">

Places don't move automatically when you raise the limit or when you remove someone from the guest list. In those cases, give the free places to people on the waitlist yourself with **Give a spot**.

</Callout>

## Approving each sign-up

Turn on **Approve each sign-up** (under **Who can find it?**) when you want to choose who comes. Everyone who signs up then asks to join and waits under **To approve** on the **Guests** tab, even when there are places left. They get an email saying their request is pending.

<Steps>

1. Open the **Guests** tab and choose the **To approve** filter.
2. Choose **Answers** on a row to read what they told you.
3. Choose **Approve** to give them a place, or **Decline** to turn them down.

</Steps>

Approved guests get their ticket by email; declined guests get an email saying so. If you change your mind, **Re-accept** on a declined guest gives them a place if there's room, or puts them on the waitlist if not.

With approval turned on and a place limit set, you can only approve while places are free. Nobody is promoted from the waitlist automatically on these events; you decide every place.

Approval doesn't apply to invite-only events: invited people get a place when they sign up. See [Invite-only events](/help/registration/invitations).

## Closing registration

Under **Close sign-ups**, pick one rule:

| Option | Sign-ups close |
| --- | --- |
| **At the end** | When the event ends. |
| **Before start** | A set number of minutes, hours or days before the start. This moves with the start time if you change it. |
| **Set time** | At a date and time you choose, in the event's time zone. It must be on or before the start. |

The line under the options tells you exactly when sign-ups will close. After that, the event page shows **Registration closed** and nobody can sign up. Whatever you choose, sign-ups always close when the event ends.

You can still add guests by hand from the **Guests** tab after sign-ups close. See [The guest list](/help/guests/guest-list#adding-a-guest-yourself).

## Showing how many are going

**Show how many are going** shows the number of sign-ups on the event page. It's on for new events; turn it off if a low number early on might put people off.

## Good to know

- Taking an event **Offline** or back to **Draft** stops new sign-ups straight away, but guests keep their places. See [Publishing and visibility](/help/events/publishing).
- Approvals and **Give a spot** only work while the event is live and hasn't ended.
- A guest added by hand follows the same rules: they go to the waitlist if the event is full, or to **To approve** if you approve each sign-up.
- Recurring series set their limit and approval rules on the series, and each date has its own places. See [Recurring series](/help/events/recurring-series).
