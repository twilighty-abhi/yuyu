An invite-only event is open only to the people you choose. You add their email addresses to the event's invite list; Yuyu emails each of them, and only they can open the event page and sign up. Everyone else gets a "page not found".

Use it for private dinners, member-only sessions, or anything where you know exactly who should come.

## Making an event invite-only

<Steps>

1. Open the event, choose the **Event page** tab, then **Details**.
2. In the **Publishing** card, under **Who can find it?**, choose **Invited people only**.
3. Choose **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom of the screen.

</Steps>

Invite-only events aren't listed on your organisation page, Discover or search. For the other options, see [Publishing and visibility](/help/events/publishing).

<Callout kind="permission">

Making an event invite-only is for organisation owners and admins. Managing the invite list is open to owners and admins, and to co-organisers with the **Attendee invites** permission.

</Callout>

## Inviting people

<Steps>

1. Open the **Guests** tab and choose **Invites**.
2. Type an email address in the box, for example ada@example.org.
3. Choose **Add**.

</Steps>

Each address you add gets an email titled "You're invited" with a link to the event page, asking them to register with that address. The person appears in the list with the date you invited them.

<Screenshot name="guests-invites" alt="The Invites sub-tab with the email box and a list of invited addresses" />

<Callout kind="tip">

Make the event live before you add invites. The invitation goes out straight away, and the link only works once the event is live.

</Callout>

Addresses are matched without regard to capitals, so Ada@Example.org and ada@example.org are the same invite. Adding an address that's already on the list shows "That email is already invited."

## How invited people sign up

Because anyone could type someone else's email address into a form, invite-only events need an account:

1. The invited person signs in to Yuyu, or creates an account, using the **same email address** you invited.
2. They verify that address if they haven't already.
3. They open the event page and register as usual.

Invited people get a place straight away; there's no approval step. If you've set a place limit and the event is full, they join the waitlist like anyone else. See [Capacity, waitlists and approvals](/help/registration/capacity-and-waitlist).

If someone says they can't see the page, check that they're signed in with the address on your list. A different address, or one that isn't verified yet, sees "page not found". For their side of things, see [Registering for an event](/help/attending/registering).

## Removing an invite

Choose the bin icon next to the address, then **Remove invite**. That person can no longer open the event page or sign up.

Removing an invite doesn't cancel a registration they've already made. To take their place away too, remove them on the **People** sub-tab; see [The guest list](/help/guests/guest-list).

## Finding someone in a long list

Use **Search invited emails** to find an address. The list is split into numbered pages, and the line underneath shows how many invites match.

## Good to know

- Once the event has ended, you can't add new invites.
- You can also add invites on an event that isn't invite-only. Those people get the same invitation email, which is a handy way to send the link to a few people, but anyone with the link can still sign up.
- Team registration on an invite-only event works through join links: each invited person signs in and joins their team with the link. Team leaders can't add members by name and email on these events. See [Setting up team registration](/help/registration/team-registration).
- Recurring series have their own invite list on the series page. See [Recurring series](/help/events/recurring-series).
