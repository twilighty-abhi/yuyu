Quick answers to the questions people ask most. If yours isn't here, try [Common problems](/help/troubleshooting/common-problems) or search the guide.

## For guests

### Does it cost anything?

No. Every event on Yuyu is free to attend. Yuyu has no ticket sales or payments, so nobody will ask you for card details through it.

### Do I need an account to register?

Usually not. For most events you enter your name and email, answer the organiser's questions, and your ticket arrives by email. Two exceptions:

- **Invite-only events** only open for the people who were invited. Sign in with an account using the email address your invitation went to, and verify that address.
- Some features, such as finding a team or seeing all your tickets in one place, work best when you're signed in.

An account also fills in your details for you and keeps your tickets and certificates together under **Account** → **Registrations**. See [Registering for an event](/help/attending/registering).

### Where is my ticket?

In the confirmation email, and on your **Registrations** page if you registered while signed in. If you registered as a guest on this device, the event page may also offer to show your ticket again. Still stuck? See [Common problems](/help/troubleshooting/common-problems#finding-your-ticket).

### Can I change my answers?

Not yourself. Each person can register once per event, so ask the organiser: they can edit your registration and answers from their guest list.

### How do I cancel?

Open your ticket and choose **Can't make it? Cancel registration**, or choose **Cancel registration** next to the event on your **Registrations** page. Your place goes to the next person on the waitlist, if there is one.

You can't cancel once you've been checked in, after the event has ended, or if your registration was declined. Cancelling deletes your registration and ticket, and it can't be undone, but you can register again later if there's still space.

### I got an email asking "Are you still coming?"

The organisers want to know who is still coming so they can plan. Choose **Yes, I'll be there** or **No, I can't make it** in the email, then send your answer on the page that opens. See [Answering "Are you still coming?"](/help/attending/still-coming).

### Can I give my ticket to someone else?

Ask the organiser. If you registered as a guest, without an account, the organiser can change the name and email on your registration so the ticket moves to the other person; the ticket link stays the same, so forward it to them. A registration made while signed in to an account can't be moved. In that case cancel yours, and the other person registers themselves.

### What happens if the event is full?

The button changes to **Join the waitlist**. If someone cancels, the first person on the waitlist gets their place automatically and an email with their ticket. Organisers can also move people up by hand.

### Why does it say "pending approval"?

The organiser reviews every sign-up for this event. You'll get an email when they approve or decline it.

### Who can see my details?

The organisers of the event you registered for can see your name, email and answers, and so can the people who run their organisation. They can also export the guest list. Feedback marked as anonymous is stored without your identity. The person who runs this Yuyu server can see account details when helping with support. Yuyu shows no ads and uses no trackers.

### Can I get a copy of my data, or delete my account?

Signed-in users can choose **Download my data** on **Account** → **Registrations**. There's no button to delete your account yourself: contact the person who runs this Yuyu server, or the event's organiser for anything about a specific event. See [Your data and memberships](/help/account/your-data).

## For organisers

### Can I charge for tickets?

No. Yuyu is built for free community events only.

### Who in my team can do what?

That depends on their role. Owners and admins create, publish and run events, and invite people. Only the owner changes roles, manages API access and can delete the organisation. Members can't create or publish events. Co-organisers get exactly the permissions you give them on one event. See [Roles and permissions](/help/organisations/roles).

### Why can't I create an event?

Two common reasons: you haven't verified your email yet (Yuyu says **Verify your email before creating events.**), or your role in the organisation doesn't allow it. See [Common problems](/help/troubleshooting/common-problems#missing-buttons-or-permissions).

### Can guests register without an account?

Yes, unless the event is invite-only. Guests who register without an account get their ticket by email.

### Can I edit someone's registration or move their ticket?

Yes. On the guest list, open the guest's **More actions** menu and choose **Edit registration or transfer ticket**. For guests who signed in to register, you can view the registration but not move it to someone else. **Copy ticket link** in the same menu copies their ticket address if they've lost the email.

### How do I find out who is still coming?

From the event's **Emails** tab, owners and admins can send an attendance confirmation that asks every confirmed guest to answer Yes or No. Nothing goes out until you choose to send it. To reach one guest by text or chat instead, open their menu in **Guests** → **People** and choose **Share attendance link**. Answers appear on the **Emails** card, as chips in **Guests** → **People**, on the **Overview** tab and in the guest CSV. See [Attendance confirmation](/help/guests/attendance-confirmation).

### Does check-in work without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Before the doors open, choose **Make available offline** on the check-in desk. See [Checking in offline](/help/check-in/offline).

### Can I leave an organisation?

Yes, from **Account** → **Organisations**, choose **Leave organisation**. The owner can't leave: ownership can't be handed over yet, so the only way out for an owner is to delete the organisation.

### How do I connect Yuyu to other tools?

Open the organisation's **Settings**. Owners and admins can set up **Webhooks**; only the owner sees **API access**. The guide's *Connecting other tools* section appears for owners and admins once they sign in.
